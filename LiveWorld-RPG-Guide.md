# LiveWorld RPG 1.0 - Player's Guide

## Getting started

- LEFT CLICK: on the ground it walks; on a monster it targets it and starts AUTO-ATTACK with your WEAPON (a white swing arc in melee, a plain white shot at range; the label 'WEAPON ATTACK' shows above the hotbar). Left click never casts a spell. SPELLS are the number keys 1-0 and Shift+1-6: each cast shows a turning rune circle at your feet, a coloured bolt or crescent and a burst where it lands, and the label 'SPELL: name' above the hotbar. Weapon = white, spell = colour.
- Click the ground to walk, or use W A S D / arrow keys. Click a monster to target it; Space or E attacks the nearest one.
- Hold the left mouse button and drag to look around: the view scrolls with the mouse and eases back to your hero when you move.
- BLINK (mage) and Demonic Circle (warlock) jump you forward in the direction you are running, then give +90% run speed for 8 seconds.
- RIVERS AND BRIDGES: wherever a road crosses a river or stream there is now a wooden bridge with hand rails - follow the roads to cross; you cannot wade through deep water. Rivers show flowing current streaks along their course.
- When you cast, the spell name and its type (attack or defensive) appear in a banner above the hotbar and float over your hero in the spell colour. ATTACK spells (damage) show a projectile or burst; defensive ones show a ring, bubble or heal glow.
- Keys 1-0 use your class skills; Shift+1-6 cast the six high-level skills that unlock at levels 30, 40, 50, 60, 70 and 80 (their row sits above the hotbar and shows the unlock level until then). Every level gives 3 stat points and 1 spell point; spend them at a Skill Shop. H health potion, J mana potion (maximum mana grows by 6 every level; mana comes back on its own while you are out of combat (quickest when you stand still), but not during a fight unless you equip the passive MEDITATION (unlocks at level 50, Passives tab of a Skill Shop) - it regenerates mana everywhere, even in combat, and Intellect, gear, jewels and Sunwine make it faster - so carry Mana Potions), T teleport scroll, Y buff scroll, V eat food, Z pick up loot, G fish.
- F talks to the person next to you (quest givers, kings and the buildings: shops, the Trade Market, the Stable), gathers herbs, enters dungeons and casts a fishing line at the water.
- I bag and equipment, C class, K skills and spell ranks, U character stats, M map (double-click a zone to open it, wheel zooms, drag scrolls), Q quest log, X mount / dismount, F1 this guide, F11 full screen.
- The buttons at the bottom: Bag I, Skills K, Hero C, Stats U, Map M, Quest Q, Guide F1 - and three more: GEAR (only what you wear, with its power, bonuses and sockets, plus your gear totals), MATERIALS (your cloth, leather, ore, dust and essence, where each comes from, and your elixirs - click one to drink it) and PARTY (your recruited companions). The BAG tab shows your pack plus the character and its gear slots; the Gear and Mats tabs show only their own list.
- The gear button in the bottom-right corner (or Esc) opens Settings: graphics, brightness, sound, full screen and minimize.
- Graphics come in three levels: Lite (very slow computers), Normal and High (sharpened picture, finer lighting, soft shadows, more particles and sun rays at dawn and dusk). F9 steps through them. Fast PCs start on High, and the game lowers the level by itself if it starts to stutter.
- Day and night follow the clock. Nights are moonlit; raise Brightness in Settings if you want it lighter.
- Your world saves every 30 seconds and whenever you quit - also if the computer logs out or the game is stopped from a terminal. Only one copy of the game can run at a time; starting it again just tells you it is already open.

## The world

- The world is huge: 1440 by 1200 tiles, twenty zones, forty towns and cities and twenty hamlets - crossing from one zone to the next is a real journey (buy a mount!), with rivers you cross on the roads' bridges. Zones are mixed: every zone holds both Alliance and Horde towns, with quests for your own side - only the two capital zones (Greenfields, Ashen Vale) belong to one faction and are hostile ground for the other side - and every Alliance town is hostile to the Horde and the other way round, in any zone - and every raid and the heroic raid has an entrance on each side. Roads link every city; milestones and waystations stand along them.
- ROAMERS: 100 heroes (50 Alliance, 50 Horde, levels 10-100, any class, Poor to Mythic gear) wander the whole map. Now and then - never more than one at a time, and at most once every 40 to 80 minutes - a strong one marches on an enemy town and fights its guards; if it slays the ruler the town falls and cannot be attacked again for 24 hours. The longer a city battle drags on, the likelier it is that higher-level heroes ride in to help defend. A red alert banner shows whenever one of your side's towns is attacked. The 20 hamlets are tiny villages with a thin guard; capitals have by far the strongest defence.
- Settlements come in three sizes. TOWNS are small hamlets. CITIES are walled and ruled by a King or Queen. Each faction has one CAPITAL (Alliance: Lionsreach in Greenfields; Horde: Grimhold in Ashen Vale) ruled by a High King or High Queen.
- Inside every wall you will find a Quest Giver (notice board), the ruler on a dais - and labelled buildings you simply click: the Trade Market (buy cheap, sell dear), the Blacksmith (weapons and armor to buy), the Magic Shop (enchanted Rare and Epic weapons, armor and jewelry for the zone's levels), the General Store (travel scrolls, bait, food and crafting materials), the Apothecary (potions, herbs and buff scrolls), the Forge (craft your own gear - it does not sell anything), the Fishing Hut (tackle and the fish market), the Wine Press, the Inn (alchemy and cooking), the Jeweler (rings and necklaces) with its Jeweler's Kiln, the Bank (your Vault), the Stable (riding and mounts), the Skill Shop and the War Hall.
- Guards: the ruler's Royal Guard stands around the dais, Gate Guards hold every gate in pairs and City Patrols walk the streets. They keep monsters out, defend the town in city raids and attack outlaws and anyone who attacks their town's leaders.
- Quest givers and rulers of ENEMY towns can be slain - during a city raid, or alone if you are strong enough (they are as tough as the top of their town's level band, and their guards come for you). A fallen town loses its leaders too. A successor arrives 30 minutes later. Killing one outside a city raid makes you an outlaw.
- Monsters never enter city walls (an enemy town's City Watch is another matter - see Alliance and Horde). Outside, each zone has dens where packs gather - the zone bands tell you where you belong (green = easy, yellow = fair, red = dangerous).

## Levelling and quests

- Levels come slowly now. You need kills, quests and dungeons to progress. The yellow bar above the skill bar shows your experience.
- Quest Givers stand beside the notice board in every settlement (look for the name '(Quests)'). Press F next to them to see today's work. New offers appear every few in-game days.
- You can carry 6 quests. Types: Cull (kill a species in a zone), Herbalist (gather herbs), Fresh Catch (fish), Break the Lair (dungeon bosses) and Champion Hunt (elite monsters). While you hunt, the zone keeps about ten of your quarry within reach of you (white dots on the minimap), and monsters that die come back - in the open world, never in dungeons or raids.
- Progress is tracked automatically and shown on the right side of the screen. When a quest is complete, walk back to the town that gave it and press F next to its quest giver to turn it in for gold, a large chunk of XP, a piece of gear and bonus items.
- Every quest pays a gear piece for your class, for the slot you are weakest in: ordinary work gives Uncommon or Rare gear, Champion Hunts, Daily quests and Break the Lair give Rare or Epic, and royal commissions from kings give Epic (sometimes Elite). On top come Health and Mana Potions, and the bigger jobs add Spirit Essence, Health Bread, a buff scroll or a Legendary Essence for gear upgrades. The reward is listed on every quest before you accept it.
- Kings and queens offer Royal Commissions: harder jobs that pay three times as much.
- QUEST MARKERS: every active quest is marked on the maps. QUEST AREAS: on the world map (M), the zoomed map and the minimap every active quest shows a soft pulsing GOLD AREA where it can be done - circles round the dens of the monsters you must hunt (the real places they are), a circle at the dungeon for 'Break the Lair', and the whole zone outlined for herb and fish quests. On the minimap the gold area is shown without any '?' dot (the big dots were misleading); the monsters, herbs and ore your quests need are drawn as WHITE dots, and a gold pointer on the rim shows the way when a quest area is off the dial. The '?' marks stay on the world map and the zoomed map: hover one for the quest name and count. Hover a mark for the quest name and count. Hover a quest offer at a Quest Giver or a Mayor, a quest in the tracker (right of the screen) or in the Quest log (Q) for its full description, progress, where to go and the reward.
- Quest log (Q) lists distance and direction to the zone and the town to return to.
- Each zone has its own flavour: caravan guards in the Sunscar Desert, lagoon fishing and jungle remedies on the Storm Coast and in the Bloodfen, mountain patrols in Frostmarch, the Ember Highlands and Wyrmreach.
- Journey quests: finish all the regular work in every town of a zone (and its Legendary questline) and the quest givers there offer one Journey quest. It sends you to the next zone up and pays Rare or Epic gear - hand it in at the town you are sent to. Follow the journeys and you work through the game zone by zone.
- Daily quests: speak to the Daily Quest Giver (blue ! marker) standing beside the regular Quest Giver. Each zone near your level has one repeatable job, available again 24 real hours after you hand it in, with bonus potions, Spirit Essence and a Teleport Scroll on top of the gear piece.
- Royal guards stand around every ruler. Each city raid finds the city differently defended - sometimes a full garrison, sometimes almost nobody - so rally to the walls when the garrison is thin. The WAR HALL has two tabs: Battlegrounds (PvP) and Raids (call a city raid for 500 gold, see the besieged city and the nearest raid dungeons). Stations in every city: the FORGE (weapons and armour up to Legendary), the FISHING HUT (rods, bait, fish-oil health and mana potions) and the WINE PRESS (potions and Sunwine). Click the building to walk over and open it, or press F beside it.
- Health Bread (Inn, Cooking: 3 herbs + 1 fish) and Sunwine (Wine Press) are used with Y: bread gives bonus Strength, Agility, Intellect and Vitality for 3 minutes.

## Gear by class

- Armor follows World of Warcraft rules. Each class wears exactly one armor type and wields only its own weapon types; the Bag marks unusable items in red and equipping them is refused.
- Mage: cloth armor; weapons - staff, wand, dagger.
- Warlock: cloth armor; weapons - wand, staff, dagger, grimoire.
- Rogue: leather armor; weapons - dagger, sword.
- Druid: leather armor; weapons - staff, mace, claws, scepter.
- Monk: leather armor; weapons - staff, fist, claws.
- Ranger: leather armor; weapons - bow, dagger.
- Shaman: mail armor; weapons - totem, axe, mace, staff.
- Cleric: mail armor; weapons - mace, scepter, staff.
- Berserker: mail armor; weapons - axe, sword.
- Warrior: plate armor; weapons - sword, axe, mace, hammer.
- Paladin: plate armor; weapons - sword, mace, hammer, axe.
- Juggernaut: plate armor; weapons - hammer, mace, axe, sword.
- Loot, shops and the forge only offer gear your class can use. Epic and better drops are bound to your class.
- Set gear: every set is SIX pieces - weapon, armor, helm, necklace and two rings - with bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 pieces that add up. Sets come from raids (raid Regalia), the Magic Shop (zone sets), the War Hall Armory (PvP Battlegear) and the Forge. You can wear pieces of several sets at once. See 'Gear sets and upgrades'.

## Gathering

- Herbs: glowing wild plants grow across every zone (green squares on the minimap). Walk up and press F, or click the plant. Each zone has its own herb; it regrows after a few minutes.
- Mining: rocks with coloured metal seams are ore veins (orange squares on the minimap) - Copper and Iron in the low zones, Silver, Gold and Mithril higher up. Press F next to one, or click it. You get Iron Ore; Silver, Gold and Mithril veins also give Arcane Dust and sometimes Spirit Essence.
- Skinning: beasts you kill (wolves, boars, bears, spiders, yetis, scorpions, drakes, basilisks...) leave a carcass with a pulsing ring for two minutes. Press F or click it for Leather Scrap; elites can give Spirit Essence.
- Cloth Scrap and Iron Ore also drop from humanoids (bandits, orcs, kobolds...), Arcane Dust from undead and magical creatures, Spirit Essence from elites and dungeon bosses. The Apothecary sells Cloth, Leather, Iron Ore and Arcane Dust if you are short.
- HOW TO GET CLOTH: (1) Cloth Scrap drops from humanoid monsters - bandits, goblins, orcs, kobolds, cultists - about half of their drops; (2) buy it at the General Store (the grocer in every town); (3) WEAVE it at an Inn: Alchemy tab, 'Weave Cloth x3' turns any 3 herbs into 3 Cloth Scrap; (4) fish up sunken caches. Cloth is needed for cloth armor (Mage, Warlock, Cleric), Scroll of Swiftness, and for the invisibility, dodge and speed brews.
- Leather Scrap: skin beast carcasses (F), buy it at the General Store, or find it in sunken caches. Every fish you catch and every herb, ore or hide you gather pops up on a card at the right of the screen with its picture. Everything you gather is saved with your character: materials live in your pack and are listed on the Mats tab (the Mats button on the bottom bar, or Bag > Mats).
- Fish: stand by any water and press G (see Fishing).
- What it is for: herbs -> potions and scrolls at the Inn; fish -> food at the Inn; ore, leather, cloth, dust and essence -> weapons, armor, helms and necklaces at the Forge; ore, dust and gold -> jewels, rings and necklaces at the Jeweler's Kiln.
- JEWELS: cut Rubies (Strength), Emeralds (Agility), Sapphires (Intellect), Topaz (Vitality) and Diamonds (critical strike) at a Jeweler's Kiln - Chipped (level 1), Flawed (15), Clean (30), Flawless (50) and Perfect (75). Any gear can be socketed at the kiln: the 1st socket needs level 10, 8 Iron Ore, 2 Arcane Dust and 250 gold; the 2nd level 30, 20 Ore, 6 Dust, 1 Spirit Essence and 1,500 gold; the 3rd level 55, 40 Ore, 15 Dust, 3 Essence and 6,000 gold. Then set jewels into the sockets (prying one out destroys it). The kiln also turns a jewel into a ring (1.5x its bonus) or a necklace (2x). You wear one necklace and two rings.

## Crafting

- Materials drop from monsters straight into your pack: Cloth Scrap and Iron Ore from humanoids, Leather Scrap from beasts, Arcane Dust from undead and magical creatures, Spirit Essence from elites and dungeon bosses. The Apothecary also sells scraps, ore and dust.
- Alchemist (the Inn): health and mana potions and the Scroll of Teleportation from herbs; Scrolls of Might (+20% damage), Fortitude (-20% damage taken) and Swiftness (+25% speed) from herbs plus a material. Herbs grow wild - press F beside a glowing plant. Press Y to read a buff scroll.
- ELIXIRS AND POTIONS (Inn or Fishing Hut, 'Elixirs' tab): critical strike, defense, dodge, invisibility, stat, speed, regeneration and flask brews - see the Elixirs and potions page. Each is made from the herb AND the fish of one zone band, so higher zones give stronger recipes.
- Cooking (the Inn): turn fish and herbs into Grilled Fish, Herbed Fish Stew and Angler's Feast.
- The Forge: Fine, Superior, Exquisite and Masterwork (level 50) weapons, armor, helms and necklaces for your class, then Heroic (Elite, 60), Grandmaster (Elite, 75), Legendary (90) and Ascendant (level 100 - stronger than Legendary) pieces. Higher tiers need more materials, Arcane Dust, Spirit Essence and gold. The item power is set by your level when you craft it.

## Fishing and health food

- Stand beside any lake, pond or river and press G (or F). Wait for the float to dip - 'A fish is biting!' - then press G again before it gets away. Moving or fighting cancels the cast, and you cannot cast while in combat: you stay 'in combat' for about 5 seconds after the last monster stops chasing or hitting you (monsters give up the chase beyond 20 tiles, 30 for aggressive ones), so fish away from monsters. The answer to G (no water close enough, cannot fish while fighting, a bite) shows on screen above the hotbar.
- Every ten catches raises your fishing skill, which gives you a longer window to react. Each zone has its own fish; Giant fish give two, and rarely you hook a Golden Carp worth gold.
- REWARDS: every catch gives a little XP; about one cast in sixteen brings up a SUNKEN CACHE with gold and crafting materials (sometimes a Spirit Essence), Giant fish count as two, and Golden Carp pay gold. Fish are also the key ingredient of every elixir.
- FISH PER ZONE: Meadow Trout (levels 1-10), Ironwood Pike (11-20), Frost Char (21-30), Storm Mackerel (31-40), Fen Eel (41-50), Crown Carp (51-60), Dusk Catfish (61-70), Sun Perch (71-80), Ember Koi (81-90), Void Angler (91-100). Each potion recipe names the exact fish and herb it needs.
- Cook fish at the Inn, then press V out of combat to eat: you recover a percentage of your health every second while you stay out of combat.

## The Trade Market

- Every settlement has a Trade Market - a labelled building with a striped awning on the south side of the square. Click it, or press F beside it.
- Prices differ from town to town and drift every couple of days. Buy where something is cheap and sell where it is dear. Selling a lot of one good lowers its price in that town for a while.
- You can buy and sell potions, herbs, materials, fish, food, scrolls and gear. Rare gear listings rotate and are sold by travelling traders.

## Dungeons and raids

- Dungeons (4 players) and raids (12 players) have entrances outside the cities. Press F at the entrance. Your tank, healer and damage dealers are AI heroes who fight beside you.
- Leaving: when the final boss falls a portal opens. At any other time press O (or the Leave button) while out of combat to open an exit portal and step through it.
- DUNGEONS: the party does not stop after ordinary fights - the tank moves straight on to the next room. After each BOSS fight in a dungeon it waits 15 seconds so you can heal, eat and pick up loot (press N or the Ready button to skip). RAIDS keep their 60-second rest after every cleared room.
- FARMING: yes, both are farmable. There is no lockout and no daily limit - every time you walk in, the dungeon is freshly built with new monsters and a new boss. Clear it as often as you like.
- What a run gives. Trash packs sometimes drop gear. Every boss drops 1 item (dungeon) or 2 items (raid / heroic) that fall on the floor: press Z to pick them up. Dungeon loot is mostly Rare and Epic with a rare Elite or Legendary; raid loot is mostly Epic with some Elite and a rare Legendary; heroic loot is Epic or better. Epic and better drops are bound to your class. Clearing the last boss also pays gold and XP (raid: about 2x a dungeon, heroic: about 6x).
- Every full raid or heroic clear gives 1 Raid Token and 1 Legendary Essence, and every boss gives 1-2 Spirit Essence. The final boss of a raid drops a piece of its class set in about one clear in three (rarely two pieces) and has a rare chance at a Legendary weapon or armor. Tokens and Essence are spent upgrading gear at the War Hall Armory.
- Best farming: level-appropriate raids for set pieces, Raid Tokens and Essence; dungeons for Epic gear and Spirit Essence; the level 91+ Heroic raid for Elite and Legendary drops, the Mythic weapon quest and - rarely, about one clear in fourteen - a piece of MYTHIC raid armor (Sovereign's cuirass, crown, pendant or signet, power 200+). Gear is a grind: expect around 25 clears of a raid to complete its set.

## Gear sets and upgrades

- POWER ORDER: raid set gear is the best armor, helm and necklace in the game, ahead of Legendary, Elite and crafted gear. Raid set weapons are the best weapons you can get from raids - but the MYTHIC weapon of your class beats every other weapon, even a fully upgraded raid weapon.
- SIX-PIECE SETS. A set has six pieces: Weapon, Armor, Helm, Necklace and two Rings, all class-locked. Wear 2, 4 or 6 pieces to switch on the 2-, 4- and 6-piece bonuses - they add up, so six pieces give all three. Bonuses show in the Stats window (U), in the Bag tooltip (lit when active) and on the shop and Armory tooltips; pieces change how your hero looks.
- RAID SETS (Regalia): the final boss of every raid can drop a piece of its own themed set (about one clear in two): 2 pieces +8% damage, +4% max health; 4 pieces +10% damage, +6% max health and a boost to your main stat; 6 pieces +5% critical strike, 8% less damage taken, +7% damage and +10% healing. The Heroic raid sets (Voidtouched) are 25% stronger. Raid gear is never sold - you have to earn it.
- PVP SETS (Battlegear): each Armory tier (Recruit's, Gladiator's, Legendary Gladiator's, Mythic Gladiator's) is its own six-piece set: 2 pieces +Vitality and 5% less damage taken; 4 pieces +8% damage and +main stat; 6 pieces +10% max health, +4% dodge, +4% critical strike. Higher tiers scale the bonuses up.
- MAGIC SHOP SETS: the Magic Shop has a tab 'Zone Set (6)' with its zone's six-piece set for your class (Meadowguard in Greenfields up to Wyrmscale and Nightveil in the top zones): 2 pieces +main stat; 4 pieces +damage, +max health; 6 pieces +critical strike, +damage, +Vitality. The higher the zone, the stronger the set.
- MAGIC SHOP GEAR: the Magic Shop sells the best shop gear in the game - Rare, Epic and Elite pieces for the zone's levels, each with a BONUS ('of the Tiger': +Strength and +Agility; 'of the Owl': +Intellect and mana regeneration; 'of Slaying', 'of Precision', 'of the Bastion', 'of Evasion' and more), some already upgraded to +1 or +2, and Epic or better with a socket. It is stronger than anything the Blacksmith or the Jeweler sells, but still below raid and Mythic gear.
- WHERE TO GET SETS. Raid Regalia from raid bosses; Battlegear at the War Hall; zone sets at any Magic Shop. Dungeons drop really good gear too - Epic, Elite and the odd Legendary.
- THE ARMORY. Press F at the War Hall and open the Armory tab. PvP GEAR comes in four tiers for your class, all bought with PvP Points: Recruit's (Rare, level 10+, 80-150 points a piece - cheap), Gladiator's (Elite, level 20+, 300-600), Legendary Gladiator's (level 60+ and 25 battleground wins, 1,300-2,400) and Mythic Gladiator's (level 90+ and 60 wins, 3,500-6,000 - a long grind, power 196, weapon 204). Every tier has a weapon, armor, helm, necklace and ring.
- BATTLE MOUNTS (Armory tab): the only mounts allowed inside battlegrounds, each with powers while you ride - Recruit's Battle Wolf (300 points; Second Wind: heal 2% a second), Gladiator's Charger (1,200, 10 wins; Barding: 25% less damage), Vicious Sabertooth (2,000, 20 wins; Battle Charge: your first attack after dismounting hits 50% harder), Bloodsand Courser (3,500, 35 wins; Flag Runner: keep riding with a flag) and the Mythic Gladiator's Nightmare (9,000, 80 wins; every power, and it never throws you). They need the matching riding skill and work in the open world too.
- SUPPLIES (Armory tab): potions, teleport scrolls, elixirs, a Flask of Supreme Power and Legendary Essence for PvP Points - good for spending small amounts between big purchases. Upgrade: improve gear you already own (see below).
- FORGE UPGRADES TO +5: any piece of gear - shop, crafted, dropped, set or Mythic - can be upgraded at the Forge (tab 'Upgrade +5'), +1 to +5. Each step adds 6 power and 10% of the item's own bonus, and costs ore (or Arcane Dust for rings and necklaces), more dust and Spirit Essence from +3, plus gold; +4 and +5 also need Legendary Essence. The War Hall's Upgrade tab still upgrades Epic and better pieces for Essence and Raid Tokens.
- THE UPGRADE PATH. Common to Rare from shops and early loot -> Epic from dungeons -> Set pieces (Forge level 50, raid bosses) -> Elite and Heroic Forge gear, Gladiator gear -> Ascendant sets at level 80 -> Legendary (Forge level 90 or a rare raid drop) -> upgrade your best pieces to +5 -> the Mythic weapon at level 91+.
- WHERE TO EARN THE CURRENCIES. Raid Tokens and Legendary Essence: 1 each per full raid or heroic clear and per city raid won. PvP Points: 120 per 12v12 battleground win and 240 per 24v24 win (24 / 48 for a loss). Your totals are shown at the bottom of the Bag (I) and at the top of the Armory.
- SKILL SHOP: every town has one (purple roof, crystal spire, labelled Skill Shop). Click it to open four tabs. Spells: raise any unlocked skill up to rank 10 for 1 spell point plus gold (ranks 2-5 give +10% power and -4% cooldown each, ranks 6-10 +6% and -2%). Hotbar: click two slots to swap their skills, so any skill can sit on any key (free). Passives: each class has 7 passives (levels 1, 15, 25, 35, 50, 50 and 70 - one of the level 50 ones is Meditation, which regenerates mana); you have 1 passive slot up to level 50, 2 from level 51 and 3 from level 75, and changing one costs gold. Stats: spend stat points for a little gold. Reset gives every point back for gold.
- WHERE ARE MY STATS? Press U, or click the Stats U button on the row of buttons at the bottom of the screen (next to Bag, Skills and Hero). It shows Strength, Agility, Intellect and Vitality (spend your 3 points per level at a Skill Shop), maximum health, damage bonus, crit, run speed, mana cost and regeneration, plus your equipped gear and set bonus. Hero (C) shows class and talents; Skills (K) shows spell ranks.

## Battlegrounds

- Leaving a battleground before it ends is not a loss: your rating and win/loss record do not change (you just get no reward).
- Click the War Hall or press F beside it (red-roofed building with crossed swords, in every city). Battlegrounds are 12 versus 12 or 24 versus 24, and the maps are huge: Fallen Citadel, Warring Fortresses and Fields of the Five Wells (12v12); Siege of Ashfall, The Sundered Marches and Crown of the Seven Wells (24v24, seven capture points in Domination).
- Your allies now find their way around the cover across the whole map (they path around walls instead of getting stuck), in Capture the Flag the whole team goes for the enemy flag (only the two closest chase a stolen one), and the battlegrounds are easier: your side is sturdier and hits harder, the enemy softer, and only half of the enemy team hunts you in Team Deathmatch.
- PARTY ROLES: in dungeons and raids your AI party plays like real players. Tanks taunt anything that goes after a healer or DPS, and raise a Shield Wall (half damage for 5 seconds) when low. Healers heal the most hurt member first (the tank counts extra), use big heals in emergencies and never double-heal the same target. DPS use a burst cooldown on bosses and big packs, interrupt boss casts, back away when hurt and chewed on, and drink a healing potion at 35% health. In the open world, badly hurt adventurers retreat to town, rest at the inn and go back out.
- SQUADS: battleground bots band into party-style squads - a tank leads, a healer stays close, damage dealers fight beside them - and move together between objectives. It cannot always work out, so some bots go it alone. In the open world, wandering adventurers also try to group as a tank, a healer and damage dealers before they head for a dungeon. You will not be killed by one blow in a battleground: a single hit takes at most 12% of your health and a second of hits at most 25%.
- FLAGS: picking up a flag gives you 5 seconds of grace and enemies hit a flag carrier far less hard, so you can get away. Flag runners on both sides take the flanks. Your allies and the enemy spread out across the map instead of clumping.
- The enemy and allied bots now make their own decisions: some defend their flag or point, some chase the flag carrier, some escort your carrier, healers stay with the wounded, and badly hurt bots fall back to a shrine. Allies spread across the whole map instead of marching in a line.
- Modes: Team Deathmatch (most kills), Capture the Flag (steal the enemy flag and bring it home while yours is safe) and Domination (hold capture points for score).
- Ruined walls give cover. Healing shrines (green circles) heal 6% of your health per second while you stand in them. Winning pays gold, XP, PvP points and rating, and one win in four also gives a prize item.
- Expert and Master Riding unlock FLYING mounts (Royal Gryphon, Frost Wyvern, Celestial Pegasus, Shadow Drake): they cross water and walls and monsters cannot reach you. Land on open ground to dismount. No mount works in dungeons or raids, and only battle mounts (War Hall Armory) work in battlegrounds.

## Elixirs and potions

- How it works: brew at an Inn or a Fishing Hut (Elixirs tab). Each recipe needs the herb and the fish of one zone band - higher-level recipes use the herb and fish of higher zones - plus Cloth, Leather, Arcane Dust or Spirit Essence for the stronger ones. Drink with Y (the next brew you do not already have active) or click it on the Materials tab. One effect of each kind at a time.
- Elixir of Sharp Eyes (level 5, Critical strike): +3% critical strike chance for 10 minutes. Needs 2 Meadow Clover, 1 Meadow Trout and 20 gold - makes 2.
- Elixir of Deadly Aim (level 25, Critical strike): +5% critical strike chance for 10 minutes. Needs 2 Frostpetal, 1 Frost Char, 1 Arcane Dust and 100 gold - makes 2.
- Elixir of the Mongoose (level 45, Critical strike): +8% critical strike chance for 10 minutes. Needs 3 Fenbloom, 2 Fen Eel, 2 Arcane Dust and 180 gold - makes 2.

## Jewelry

- Jewelers' Kilns (in every town) cut NINE gems in six qualities: Ruby (Strength), Emerald (Agility), Sapphire (Intellect), Topaz (Vitality), Diamond (critical strike), Onyx (dodge), Jade (less damage taken), Opal (mana regeneration) and Amber (maximum health). Chipped unlocks at level 1, Flawed 15, Clean 30, Flawless 50, Perfect 75 and Radiant 90.
- RINGS (two worn) give 1.5 times the gem's bonus, NECKLACES (one worn) give twice. Flawless and better rings and necklaces also add Vitality. Higher levels mean better jewelry - a Radiant Diamond Necklace is worth far more than a Chipped Ruby Ring.
- Jewels also go into gear sockets: up to 3 per piece, opened at the kiln for ore, dust, essence and gold. Everything you wear shows on the Gear tab.

## Monsters

- Monsters have temperaments. AGGRESSIVE ones (all elites and about a third of beasts) notice you from far away and chase you a long way. TERRITORIAL ones only attack when you come close, and only if you are not far below their level. PASSIVE ones never attack unless you hit them first - but hit one and its pack mates join in.
- Monsters roam between waypoints, stop to graze, and some hold their post. An invisibility potion makes even aggressive monsters ignore you until you are almost touching them.
- ELITES are marked: a pulsing gold ring on the ground, a gold name with a star that is always visible, and a gold marker on the minimap. They have 2.6 times the health, hit 60% harder and drop triple loot, Spirit Essence and better materials.

## Other adventurers

- The world is full of computer players - over six hundred of them, every class, levels 1 to 100, from plain Common gear to full Legendary sets. Their gear shows on them, and hovering one shows its class, level and gear.
- They live like players: they travel the roads between towns across every zone, hunt in zones that suit their level, rest at the inn at night, trade at the market, group up for dungeons, and talk. What they say appears over their heads and in the chat.
- Click anyone (or press F next to them) to see who they are - personality, what they are doing, duel record - and to greet them or challenge them to a duel. They may say no: the proud love a fight, the shy never want one, and nobody wants to fight someone far stronger.
- Some will challenge YOU. A pop-up lets you accept or decline. Duels stop at the last hit point - nobody dies - and the winner takes the wager. Yield any time with the Yield button.
- You can't simply attack the other faction: open their window and challenge them to a Fight to the death, and they accept or decline (hot-heads may also challenge you). Killing another faction's champion in the open world (not in battlegrounds, dungeons, raids or city raids) makes you an OUTLAW for 15 minutes: your name blinks red, every town guard of any faction attacks you, and enemy adventurers - plus hot-heads of your own side - may hunt you. You can fight back against anyone attacking you. Dying clears it.
- Name colours: blue is your own faction (you, its townsfolk and adventurers), orange is the other faction, and pale gold is neutral. Monsters are red.
- Friendly adventurers sometimes jump in to help when they see you fighting.
- PARTY: click an adventurer of your own faction - or a town guard - and use the tabs: OVERVIEW (who they are, duels), GEAR (what they wear and its power) and ADD TO PARTY (ask them to join). They may say yes or no: friendly, explorer and guardian types usually agree, proud and shy ones rarely do, a much higher level lowers the odds, and a refusal makes them unwilling for a while. Up to 3 companions follow you, fight what attacks you and heal you a little if they are healers. The PARTY button at the bottom lists them and lets you dismiss them.
- In dungeons and raids each companion takes the place of one AI ally - the group stays the same size, the runs themselves are unchanged.

## Alliance and Horde

- Every town, city and capital belongs to the Alliance or the Horde. You start in your own capital zone: the Alliance in Greenfields, the Horde in Ashen Vale - those two zones are hostile to the other side. Every other zone is mixed, with a town of each side - but a town of the other side is always enemy ground. Your own side's towns are always safe.
- The other side's towns are not - in any zone. Walk inside an enemy wall and its CITY WATCH comes for you; inside the other side's whole capital zone its Watch hunts you even in the open - soldiers marked 'City Watch' that fight until you leave or fall. Their shops, quest givers, Bank, Forge, Fishing Hut, Wine Press and War Hall will not serve you.
- Quests come only from your own side's quest givers, and they send you into the mixed zones around you (any zone of your level) - a zone can hold both Alliance and Horde towns, each with its own side's quests. Hand them in where you took them.
- SOLO RAID: you don't need a warband to raid a city. Walk into any town of the other side and attack its ruler, mayor or quest givers yourself: the guards turn on you, a slain leader pays gold and XP, and you become an outlaw (unless a city raid is running there). They return in 30 minutes. A level 100 city fields up to 9 tough watchmen and is a hard solo raid. A hero 15+ levels above a town can wipe it out alone: its Watch is tuned to the town's own level band, its guards fall and all its leaders die at once. Computer adventurers rest, trade and respawn in their own side's towns.
- Dying in the open world - to monsters, to the Watch or to the other faction - costs 5% of the gold you carry. Gear is never lost. Keep your savings in the Vault.

## The Vault

- Every town has a BANK (a labelled building). Click it to open your vault.
- Deposit gold and it is safe: gold in the vault is never lost when you die. Take it out again at any Bank - every Bank opens the same vault.
- Click gear in your Bag to store it, click it in the vault to take it back. The vault holds 200 items, so your Bag stays tidy. Stored gear cannot be worn or sold until you take it out; take off worn gear (Bag, I) before storing it.

## Spell effects

- Hover any skill (hotbar, Skills K, Skill Shop, Hotbar tab) to see what it does, the damage it deals with your gear, its mana cost, cooldown and range. Hover a passive or a class card for its details too.
- Elements: COLD spells (Frost Nova, Deep Freeze, Frost Shock, Avalanche Slam) slow enemies by half for 4 seconds and have a 25% chance to freeze them solid for 1.5 seconds. FIRE spells (Ignite, Meteor, Flame Shock, Lava Burst, Rain of Fire) ignite the target so it burns for 4 seconds. ELECTRIC spells (Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, Stormstrike, Thunderstorm) have a 40% chance to stun the target for 2 seconds and arc to a nearby enemy for 40% damage. EARTH spells (Earthquake, Earthshaker, Seismic Slam) stagger and slow the target for 2 seconds. SHADOW spells (Shadow Bolt, Corruption, Doom, Drain Life) wither the target: it takes 10% more damage from you for 4 seconds. HOLY spells (Smite, Judgement, Holy Shock, Consecration) heal you for 6% of the damage they deal. POISON and nature spells (Serpent Sting, Moonfire, Volley of Thorns) poison the target for 8% weapon damage a second over 4 seconds. CLASS BONUSES on every spell hit: Warrior bleeds (25%), Berserker leeches 8%, Juggernaut slows, Paladin heals 4%, Cleric heals 6%, Warlock drains 5%, Rogue exposes the target (+10% damage), Ranger hobbles (25%), Monk stuns (15%), Shaman slows (20%), Druid heals 3%, Mage echoes a 40% arcane bolt (12%). MAGES choose their element: FIRE spells (Ignite, Meteor) burn and give you Fire Mastery (+10% damage for 6 seconds); FROST spells (Frost Nova, Deep Freeze) slow and can freeze solid, and give you Frost Armor (-10% damage taken); ARCANE and LIGHTNING spells (Arcane Bolt, Arcane Missiles, Starfall, Arcane Barrage, Disintegrate, Lightning Bolt) can stun and give you Arcane Charge (+8% crit). Every class gets the same charge from its own fire, frost and lightning spells. Spend your stat and spell points - unspent points leave you weaker than you should be.

## Faction raids

- WAYGATES: the major city of every zone has a Waygate - a tower with a glowing teal ring, one of the city's own buildings. Press F beside it (or click it) to open the list of your side's cities and travel to any of them for 50 gold and one Scroll of Teleportation (brewed at the Inn, sold at the General Store). Cities under attack are listed first, so you can step straight into a raid to defend them.
- City raids now field 24 a side: the attacking warband is always full, and every wave comes at full strength. Defence is unchanged - the town's guards and the adventurers in the area rally to its walls.
- MUSTER: a city raid no longer starts the moment it is announced. A red banner across the top of the screen - visible anywhere except in dungeons, dungeon raids and battlegrounds - names the warband and the town, shows a 5-minute countdown and the warband roster (for example 7/12). Click 'Join the warband' while there is a place: you are sent to the town the moment the raid starts. The banner goes away after a minute; you can still join, or leave, at the War Hall (Raids tab) for the whole muster. Empty places are filled with volunteers, so every warband marches full (12/12). Most of the time a place is left for you; sometimes the roster fills up first - then wait, because a volunteer is usually called away and a place opens. If you do not join, the warband marches without you (you can still run there and fight). A muster that comes due while you are inside a dungeon or battleground waits until you are out.
- Now and then a warband of one faction raids a town, a city or a capital of the other - near you, or elsewhere in the world. The chat says who attacks where, and the banner at the top shows waves, raiders left, defenders and time.
- When YOUR side's town is attacked: adventurers of your faction rally to the walls beside the guards. Kill at least one raider yourself to share in the reward when the town is saved.
- When YOUR side attacks: go to the enemy town and cut down its City Watch while your warband batters the gates. If the town falls and you killed at least one of its Watch, you share the spoils (gold, XP, 1 Raid Token, 1 Essence).
- At the War Hall you can pay 500 gold to muster your side's warband against the nearest enemy town (level 10+); it marches after the same 5-minute muster and you are already in the roster. Changed your mind? Cancel the muster at the War Hall for half the gold back, or - once it has started - call off a raid you called (no win, no loss).

## Mounts

- Train riding at the Stable in any town (levels 5, 25, 40, 60) and buy a mount. Press X to mount. Being hit or attacking throws you off a ground mount. Battle mounts from the War Hall Armory can also be ridden inside battlegrounds and have special powers.

## Updating and troubleshooting

- UPDATE: install the new .deb over the old one (sudo apt install ./liveworld-rpg_<version>_all.deb, or double-click it in the software centre). Your characters and saves live in ~/.local/share/liveworld-rpg and are never touched by an update. Close the game first; it saves on exit.
- CHECK A DOWNLOAD: put the .deb and SHA256SUMS-<version>.txt in one folder and run sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS-<version>.txt - every line must say OK.
- BUILD FROM SOURCE: unpack the source .tar.gz, then run ./build.sh --test inside it. It runs the tests and writes the .deb, the source archive, the guide and the checksums to dist/.
- LOGS: the game writes ~/.local/state/liveworld-rpg/startup.log (and startup.log.1 for the run before). If the game ever closes by itself, send that file - a crash inside a library now leaves a Python traceback in it.
- NO SOUND, OR A CRASH WHEN CLICKING A BUTTON: start the game with sound switched off - LIVEWORLD_NO_AUDIO=1 liveworld-rpg - and tell us if the problem goes away. Sound is now only ever driven from the main thread, which fixes crashes some PCs had on button clicks (Accept, Decline, Turn in...).
- SLOW FIRST START: the first launch paints and caches the world map and road layout (20-35 s on a slow PC); later starts take a few seconds. The cache files in the save folder are safe to delete - they are rebuilt.
- REMOVE: run liveworld-uninstall (it asks whether to keep your saves), or sudo apt purge liveworld-rpg.

## The Mythic weapon

- At level 91 you can begin the Mythic questline. You can forge exactly ONE Mythic weapon per class; it is a red, bound, best-in-slot weapon.
- Steps: (1) Slay 150 creatures in Wyrmreach or Shadowlands. (2) Defeat the final boss of a level 91-100 dungeon or raid 6 times. (3) Defeat Ashmaw the Devourer in the Heroic raid 3 times. (4) Defeat Queen Veyra of the Hollow Choir in the Heroic raid 3 times. (5) Interrupt 25 boss casts in dungeons. (6) Defeat Malachar, the Void Sovereign 5 times. (7) Win 12 battlegrounds. (8) Help defend 4 cities from elite raids. (9) Bring 10 Spirit Essence, 25 Arcane Dust, 40 Iron Ore and 25,000 gold to the Forge.
- The final step is done at the Forge: click it when you have the materials and the gold.
- MYTHIC BONUSES: every Mythic piece has its own bonus - the Voidreaver weapon gives +40 main stat, +8% damage, +4% critical strike and a role bonus (healing, damage taken, dodge or mana regeneration); Sovereign's raid armour (Heroic raid, rare drop) and Mythic Gladiator's PvP gear carry theirs. The questline itself pays too: every finished step gives +2 to all stats, and completing it grants the OATH OF THE SOVEREIGN: +15 to all stats, +5% damage, +5% max health, +3% critical strike.
- Warrior: Voidreaver, Blade of the Sovereign
- Ranger: Voidreaver, Bow of the Sovereign
- Mage: Voidreaver, Staff of the Sovereign
- Cleric: Voidreaver, Scepter of the Sovereign
- Rogue: Voidreaver, Twin Daggers of the Sovereign
- Paladin: Voidreaver, Sword of the Sovereign
- Shaman: Voidreaver, Totem of the Sovereign
- Druid: Voidreaver, Staff of the Hollow Star
- Berserker: Voidreaver, Greataxe of the Sovereign
- Warlock: Voidreaver, Grimoire of the Sovereign
- Monk: Voidreaver, Quarterstaff of the Sovereign
- Juggernaut: Voidreaver, Warhammer of the Sovereign
- Progress is kept per class, so a new character class starts its own questline.
